Reading notes. The meander in the benchmark data is generated by a wind-direction process acting on the same Gaussian plume formula that the meander-aware correction models. The correction is therefore partly self-consistent with the data generator: the measured gain is an optimistic result for a simulated station, an upper bound for a real site, and is not evidence of real-world accuracy. Checked against the code: the truth model (`truth_model.py`, `truth_turbulence.py`) adds a stationary AR(1) wind-direction meander (default standard deviation 10 degrees, correlation time 120 s) to the hourly wind direction and evaluates `concentration_ppm`, the same Gaussian plume formula as the inversion, at every 10 s step with the Martin coefficient set; the 10-minute window mean of that series is a time average of Gaussian plumes whose axis wanders, which is the situation the correction `sigma_y,eff^2 = sigma_y^2 + (x s)^2` describes.

The sampling error of one seed is about plus or minus 0.07 (an approximate 95 % half-width for a proportion near 0.4 over 200 leak cases: 2 x sqrt(0.4 x 0.6 / 200) = 0.069); the headline is one seed per method on synthetic data.
