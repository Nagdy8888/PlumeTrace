## Look, compared with the mockup

The mockup is a rendered image of a full station with piping, tanks, fin-fan coolers, a warm
desert at sunset and a flame. The spike has one skid, one stack and a flat ground, so the
comparison is only about material and lighting quality:

- Works: tag-named nodes and anchors survive the whole chain; the sun shadow (WSW, 18 degrees) and
  the baked occlusion read correctly (dark contact under the pad and skid); the yellow handrail,
  dark skid and pad texture look like real equipment up close.
- Does not match the mockup yet: the ground is very bright and flat (gravel near white under sun
  intensity 2.5 plus the HDRI at 0.8; it reads as snow, not warm desert); the HDRI is
  `industrial_sunset_puresky`, a pale sky, so there is no warm golden-hour colour; every painted
  steel part carries the diamond-plate pattern of `metal_plate_nor_gl` (the normal map is a tread
  plate, wrong for vessel bodies); the equipment is plain boxes and cylinders (the piping,
  valves, instrument clutter and the second-level detail that make the mockup read as a
  refinery are Tasks 6 and 7, not in the spike); the AO is soft and blotchy on large faces
  because `uv.lightmap_pack` gives every face an equal cell (see risks).
- The spike scene has no plume, flame, labels or camera controls (later tasks).

## Risks and findings for the go / no-go

1. **AO quality.** `uv.lightmap_pack(PREF_CONTEXT="ALL_FACES", PREF_PACK_IN_ONE=True)` packs faces,
   not islands, into equal cells, so large faces (roof, pad top, skid deck) get a few pixels of AO
   and look blotchy. A smart-project unwrap or merging coplanar faces would fix it; this is not
   in the plan and was not tried (do not tune the spike).
2. **File size.** 1024 px textures make the spike 2.6 MB; the full station has about 8 more
   equipment types and per-item AO images, so the 15 MB budget needs 512 px AO and shared
   textures. The geometry is tiny (4.9 k triangles, meshopt): textures dominate.
3. **Integrated GPU not measured** (see the table). The scene is light (16 draw calls, 9.8 k
   triangles), so the risk is low, but the rule asks for the measurement.
4. **Material count.** Each item gets its own copy of every material for its AO image, so draw
   calls grow with items times materials (K-202 uses 5). The full station needs instancing or
   merged materials to stay under 300 calls.
5. **glTF-Transform 4.5.1 `optimize`** cannot keep empty leaf nodes except by turning prune off.
   Later tasks must also check that `--instance` (default true) does not merge tag nodes in the
   full station.
6. **Context7 was unavailable** (login expired) during the task; the Blender operator and exporter
   parameters were checked against the installed 4.5.16 RNA and add-on source, drei and R3F props
   against the installed type definitions.
